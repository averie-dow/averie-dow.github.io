System Critique

This is my critique of the Douglas Country Libraries website. It is currently too focused on the events at the libraries and distracts from the books and other resources the libraries have to offer. My critique of the current website focuses more on the books available at the lirbary and it attempts to make more books more popular by using a randomized approach to the API. It will randomly be pulling a book from a list of reccomended books. By having the randomized book reccomender, it exposes people to more diverse selections of books than they would normally pick from and allows for more exposure for authors.
