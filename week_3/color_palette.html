Identity & Culture:

My color and typography choices reflect my personal identity because I pride myself on maintaining a fun yet professional aesthetic. My previous portfolio was hot pink and it was actually one the reasons I was hired for the role. Now, I'm leaning into the orange color scheme because I have red hair. The fun font for my H1 feels most authentic to me because it has a glitzy, Broadway aesthetic to it and I am ALL about that.

Accessibility Considerations:

Accessibility requirements influenced my choiced because I realized that I will have constraints on using the orange I wanted to use originally on a white background because it does not follow WCAG AAA normal text guidelines. Normally, my work follows AA, and I'm making conscious choices to use the best version of the WCAG guidelines. Picking a new color was a challenge.

Design Process:

What suprised me the most about making a cohesive design system was how easy it fell into place after I selected my five colors. From there, as long as I had one that was light enough to act as white and one dark enough to act as black, it was very easy to pick and choose color groups. I will apply This system to my portfolio project by deciding how to design things like buttons and add them into my project.

Future Applications:

I would love to flex my HTML skills by making a dark mode version of my portfolio that uses black in place of the orange that is currently being used as a background. The least flexible aspect of my system right now feels like the text, as it is currently not as scalable as I would like it because we did not get to cover it in class.
