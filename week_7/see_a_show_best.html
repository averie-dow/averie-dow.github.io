See a Show

To see a show at Sandbox, we reccomend arriving at the theater at least one hour in advance.
This will give you enough time to ensure you get a ticket for that night's show. Sandbox can only give 45 tickets per night, as the Acting Studio is a smaller performance space.

Donate

Sandbox is entirely student run, produced, and acted. Any amount that you are able to donate means the world! To donate to CU Sandbox, find a purple bucket at the end of the show to donate cash or scan our Venmo QR code. Thank you!

Parking

We reccommend parking in the Parking lot in front of the CASE building or finding free street parking on the University Hill. These are the closest available parking locations.
